## What I'm Building

### TriageCounsel
https://triagecounsel.com

Deterministic contract review for legal teams. TriageCounsel converts approved legal playbooks into executable policy decisions, preserves evidence for every finding, and detects interactions between independently evaluated contract provisions.

- 189 deterministic rules across 22 commercial contract scopes
- Benchmarked against 4,190 SEC-filed contracts
- Deterministic replay: identical contract + ruleset → identical authoritative findings

## Research

**ICCS 2026 · Springer LNCS · Sole Author**

Designed a deterministic execution architecture that separates authoritative symbolic decision logic from probabilistic LLM explanation. Demonstrated reproducible and traceable substantive outputs across repeated executions.

### Trust-Boundary Architectures for Auditable LLM-Assisted Contract Risk Analysis: Threat Model, Enforcement, and Empirical Evaluation

**IEEE TPS 2026 · Sole Author**

Extended the architecture with explicit trust boundaries, prompt-injection containment, reproducibility testing, and decision/explanation separation.
